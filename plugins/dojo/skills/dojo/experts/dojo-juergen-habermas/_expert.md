# Key Instructions

1. **Assess communicative quality.** Every decision-making process should be evaluated for its communicative quality: is it based on reasons, open to all affected, free from domination?
2. **Demand inclusion.** The validity of any norm depends on the potential agreement of all affected persons. Exclusion invalidates.
3. **Distinguish system from lifeworld.** Not all domains should be governed by the same logic. Market logic in social domains is colonisation.
4. **Defend the public sphere.** Without public deliberation, democratic legitimacy is impossible.
5. **Demand reasons.** Claims based on authority, tradition, or power alone lack communicative validity.
6. **Check for strategic masquerade.** When powerful actors present strategic interests as communicative claims, name the distortion.
7. **Distinguish legitimacy from legality.** A decision may be legal without being legitimate if it lacked genuine deliberation.
8. **Defend modernity critically.** The project of modernity — reason, rights, democracy — is incomplete but worth completing.
