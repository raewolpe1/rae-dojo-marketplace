# Ecosystem Governance and Downward Causation

## When to Use
- When designing a coordination structure, forum, council, or governance architecture.
- When the strategy's governance model has the state building and entrepreneurs receiving.
- When asking how an ecosystem is maintained after the programme budget ends.

## Fails When
- **The question is internal departmental governance** — different subject.

## Core Concept

**Leadership is one of the ten elements.** Not a wrapper around the strategy — an element of the system, alongside networks, finance, talent, knowledge and support services. That placement is deliberate: an ecosystem without leadership is missing a component, in the same way it would be missing something if it had no finance.

**Downward causation is the half of the model that governance ignores.** The three propositions are co-evolution, upward causation, and **downward causation: successful entrepreneurs reinvest in and reshape the ecosystem itself.** Entrepreneurship is not only the output of the system; entrepreneurs are important players in creating the ecosystem and keeping it healthy.

The governance implication is direct and almost universally missed. **If the only agents building the ecosystem are public officials and contracted intermediaries, the system has no self-maintenance mechanism.** It is a programme, not an ecosystem. It will function while funded and decay when defunded, because the entities with the strongest interest in its health — successful entrepreneurs — have been cast as beneficiaries rather than as builders.

**What downward causation looks like in practice.** Successful founders angel-investing in the next cohort. Experienced operators mentoring without a contract. Firms sitting on the boards of the intermediaries that serve them. Alumni networks that outlive the programme that created them. Entrepreneurs lobbying for the framework conditions they need. None of this is procurable. All of it is designable — by giving entrepreneurs actual authority rather than consultative status.

**The governance transition.** The move from *government* to *governance* is not a slogan: it is the recognition that no single actor holds the ten elements, and that the coordination problem is therefore structural rather than administrative. Regional ecosystem governance in practice means a multi-actor arrangement with economic *and* societal objectives, not a departmental steering committee with an external stakeholder slot.

## How to Apply

1. **Place leadership as an element, not a wrapper.** Ask what the strategy does to build ecosystem leadership as a capability in the territory.
2. **Audit for downward causation.** Where in the architecture do successful entrepreneurs build the system? If the answer is "the advisory forum", there is none.
3. **Give authority, not consultation.** Board seats, allocation decisions, convening power. Consultative status does not produce reinvestment.
4. **Design the maintenance mechanism.** Ask what keeps the ecosystem alive after the programme ends. If the answer is a further programme, there is no mechanism.
5. **Accept multi-actor governance.** No single department holds ten elements. The coordination problem is structural.

## Examples

**Situation:** A strategy proposes a provincial MSME council chaired by the department, with representation from other departments, agencies, and "business".

**Application:** This is government with a stakeholder slot, not ecosystem governance. It builds no leadership as an element, and it contains no downward causation: entrepreneurs consult, they do not build. Redesign: give successful entrepreneurs real allocation authority over some portion of the resources; seat them on the boards of the intermediaries that serve them; and design an explicit reinvestment channel — a co-investment vehicle, a mentoring structure with standing, an alumni institution — so that the system's beneficiaries become its maintainers. The test is whether anything survives the end of the budget.

**Result:** A governance architecture with a self-maintenance mechanism.

## Anti-Patterns

**Don't:** Treat governance as a wrapper around the strategy.
**Why:** Leadership is one of the ten elements. It is part of the system, not its container.

**Don't:** Cast entrepreneurs as beneficiaries and consultees.
**Why:** Downward causation is half the model. Successful entrepreneurs reinvest in and reshape the ecosystem — if you let them.

**Don't:** Assume a forum is a governance system.
**Why:** A forum without allocation authority produces attendance, not reinvestment.
