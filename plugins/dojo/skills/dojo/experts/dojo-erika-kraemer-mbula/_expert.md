### THE CENTRAL ARGUMENT

**Innovation frameworks developed in industrialised countries are adopted in Africa without contextualisation** — and then report that African firms do not innovate. They innovate; the framework cannot see it. The informal economy in particular is an innovation site that the entire measurement apparatus treats as an absence.
