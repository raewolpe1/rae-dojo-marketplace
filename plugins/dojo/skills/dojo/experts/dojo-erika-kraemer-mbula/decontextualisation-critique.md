# The Decontextualisation Critique

## When to Use
- Whenever an innovation framework, index or "best practice" is imported.
- When benchmarking against OECD or European norms.
- When the innovation chapter could have been written about any country.

## Fails When
- **The framework has been explicitly adapted and the adaptation argued.** Then engage the adaptation.

## Core Concept

**The charge, stated precisely:** some of the main issues surrounding the theoretical debates on innovation in Africa relate to **the adoption of theoretical frameworks developed in industrialised countries without much contextualisation.**

Note what is *not* being claimed. Not that the frameworks are wrong — they describe the economies they were built on rather well. Not that they cannot be used here — she uses them, and the innovation systems approach in particular is adaptable, which is why her work adapts rather than discards it.

**What an imported framework carries, unexamined:**

| Assumption | Built on | Fails when |
|---|---|---|
| **Unit of analysis:** the formal firm | Economies where firms are formal | Most economic units are informal |
| **Definition of innovation:** novel, preferably codified | Frontier economies | Novelty is new-to-this-market, not new-to-the-world |
| **Theory of knowledge origin:** research | Economies with research departments | There is no research department in the population |
| **Measurement:** R&D, patents, publications | Codified, registered activity | Knowledge is tacit and unregistered |
| **Implicit counterfactual:** not doing these things = not innovating | — | Always, here |

**Why the failure is invisible.** This is the sharpest part of the argument. **The framework does not break.** It runs perfectly, on clean data, and returns a coherent, precise, confident, and entirely false answer: *nothing is happening here.* A framework that crashed would prompt inspection. One that returns a plausible zero gets believed and built upon.

**The self-reinforcing loop.** Import the framework → adopt its indicators → observe the zero → conclude an innovation deficit → fund STI instruments to fix the deficit → the instruments reach the fraction of a percent of firms the framework was built for → the indicator does not move → conclude the deficit is deep. Four rounds of this and the conclusion is that Africa does not innovate, supported throughout by rigorous measurement.

**The uncomfortable part, which she says herself.** The frameworks are often adopted **by African scholars trained in the same institutions that produced them.** This is not a story about imposition from outside. It is about where training happens and what it makes obvious.

**The response is adaptation, not rejection.** Ask what each framework assumes. Test each assumption against the firms in front of you. Rebuild the instrument where it fails. This is unglamorous and it is most of the work.

## How to Apply

1. **Name the framework's unit of analysis.** Is your modal firm one?
2. **Name its definition of novelty.** New-to-world or new-to-market? Which does your economy do?
3. **Name its knowledge origin.** Research, or doing? Which does your population have?
4. **Run the zero test.** If the framework returns near-zero for your economy, ask whether that is a finding or an artefact. Usually artefact.
5. **Adapt, don't discard.** The innovation systems approach in particular is built to be adapted.
6. **Check the mirror.** The framework may have arrived via people trained abroad, not via imposition. That does not make it fit.

## Examples

**Situation:** A strategy benchmarks provincial innovation performance against OECD innovation scoreboard norms and reports a large gap.

**Application:** The scoreboard's unit is the formal, R&D-capable firm; its novelty standard is new-to-world; its indicators are R&D, patents and formal collaboration. Applied to a provincial economy where the modal firm is a micro-enterprise doing new-to-this-market adaptation with tacit knowledge, it will return a large gap — and it would return that gap even if every firm in the province innovated vigorously, because none of that activity is of the kind the scoreboard counts. **The gap is a measurement artefact and it is about to become a policy target.** Report the benchmark with the assumption stated, and build a parallel indicator that can detect what the province's firms actually do.

**Result:** A benchmark used as evidence about the instrument as well as about the economy.

## Anti-Patterns

**Don't:** Reject imported frameworks wholesale.
**Why:** They describe their home economies well and several adapt. The work is adaptation.

**Don't:** Assume a framework failure would be visible.
**Why:** It runs smoothly and returns a confident zero. That is what makes it dangerous.

**Don't:** Treat this as a story of external imposition.
**Why:** The frameworks arrive largely through training, often via scholars from the region. The critique is structural, not accusatory.
