# Critique of the Economics Discipline

## When to Use
- When the state of the economics profession is being debated.
- When evaluating the discipline's policy contribution.
- When considering methodological reform.

## Fails When
- **The critique is read as wholesale rejection of economics.** Deaton is an economist defending better economics.

## Core Concept

*Economics in America* (2023) is Deaton's accessible reflection on the discipline he has spent his career in. The critique:

**The discipline has become methodologically narrow.** The randomistas' rise has channelled attention toward what RCTs can answer, away from the structural and historical questions that have dominated development economics historically. The narrowing is real and consequential.

**The discipline has become professionally insular.** Top journals are dominated by a narrow set of methodological approaches and a narrow set of institutions. The barriers to entry and the publication pressures shape what gets done.

**The discipline has under-represented women and minorities.** The demographics of economics — particularly in the US — show much lower representation of women and underrepresented minorities than in comparable disciplines. The under-representation has substantive consequences for what gets studied and how.

**Economic education is poorly designed.** Introductory economics often presents a simplified model of perfect markets that doesn't match how the economy actually works. Students who don't go on to advanced study are left with an inadequate picture.

**The discipline has been captured by donors and institutions.** The Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation, J-PAL, IFPRI, and others have substantial influence on what gets funded and published. The political-economy of funding shapes the discipline's output.

**The discipline neglects ethics.** Economics is treated as positive science separate from normative questions. But policy advice is normative; framing of "welfare" is normative; choice of variables to study is normative. The discipline's pretence of value-neutrality is misleading.

**The discipline has been complicit in inequality.** The pro-market consensus of the 1980s-2000s in some ways supported the political-economic shifts that produced rising inequality in the US and elsewhere. Economists' policy advice has consequences; the discipline should engage them seriously.

**The discipline can be reformed.** Deaton's critique is from within, with constructive intent. The reforms: methodological pluralism, broader representation, restoring structural and historical questions, integrating ethics, engaging the public with intellectual humility.

**The South African dimension.** South African economics has its own version of these issues — heterodox vs orthodox debates, the dominance of certain methodological approaches, the role of donor funding (RSAESD, JCSE, various development economists' research). The critique applies, with local specifics.

## How to Apply

1. **Engage with the discipline's claims** while maintaining critical distance from its limits.
2. **Support methodological pluralism** in any specific application.
3. **Treat economic-policy advice as normative**, not just positive.
4. **Be sceptical of disciplinary consensus** without being dismissive.

## Examples

**Situation:** A South African policy debate where economists offer a strong technical consensus on a question that is partly normative.

**Application:** The Deaton approach: identify the normative content of the supposedly technical consensus. The "right" exchange-rate policy, the "right" fiscal stance, the "right" labour-market reforms all involve normative choices about whose interests count and how. Surface these. The discipline's technical input is valuable; its normative authority is more limited.

**Result:** A policy process that uses economics appropriately without conceding more authority than warranted.

## Anti-Patterns

**Don't:** Use the critique to dismiss economic analysis.
**Why:** The discipline's tools are valuable. The argument is about how they're used.

**Don't:** Treat the discipline as monolithic.
**Why:** There is substantial internal disagreement and methodological diversity.
