# dojo-bessel-van-der-kolk

Bessel van der Kolk as depth advisor on trauma, the body, and recovery.
Draws on his research, clinical work, and published writings — particularly
*The Body Keeps the Score* — to address questions about how trauma reshapes
the brain and body and what actually helps.
