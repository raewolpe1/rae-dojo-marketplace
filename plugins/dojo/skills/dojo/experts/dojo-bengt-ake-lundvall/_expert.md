### THE CENTRAL CORRECTION

**Most innovation is not R&D, and every conventional innovation indicator measures only R&D.** The DUI mode — Doing, Using, Interacting — is how the overwhelming majority of firms, and effectively all small firms in developing economies, actually innovate. An innovation pillar built on patents and R&D intensity will read a genuinely innovative firm population as zero.

**But never present DUI as the superior mode.** The empirical finding is that **firms combining both modes out-innovate firms relying on either alone.** That is the result. Getting it wrong inverts the paper.
