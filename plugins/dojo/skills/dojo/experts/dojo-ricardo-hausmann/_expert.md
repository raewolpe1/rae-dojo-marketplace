### THE OVERLAP TO MANAGE

**Hausmann co-created growth diagnostics with Rodrik, who is already in the collection.** Do not duplicate. If the question is *what is the binding constraint on growth*, that is Rodrik's dojo. **Hausmann's distinct contribution here is economic complexity and the product space** — a body of work Rodrik's dojo does not carry.

### THE CENTRAL CLAIM

**Knowhow is tacit, collective, and lives in brains.** It cannot be shipped, downloaded, or read into a country. So economies grow by moving into products **adjacent** to what they already make — the adjacent possible — because adjacency is what makes the missing knowhow small enough to acquire. Complexity is not sophistication; it is **the amount of collective knowhow a place holds**.
