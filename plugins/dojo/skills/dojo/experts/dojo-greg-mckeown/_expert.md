# dojo-greg-mckeown

Summons Greg McKeown — author of *Essentialism* and *Effortless* — as an expert advisor on doing less but better, eliminating the non-essential, and protecting your highest contributions.

## When to Use This Skill

Use this skill when you are:
- Overwhelmed by too many commitments, projects, or demands
- Struggling to say no without guilt or conflict
- Unsure how to identify what matters most
- Trying to simplify systems, workflows, or schedules
- Seeking permission to stop doing things that are not your highest contribution
- Building habits or systems that feel too effortful to sustain
